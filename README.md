Minecraft Java and Bedrock have several issues due to the lack of parity between the two editions — notably differences in textures, behaviors, and more. This can be frustrating for players unfamiliar with Java or even **disrupt** the **overall gameplay experience** between the two versions.

This modpack includes only carefully selected mods, datapacks, and resource packs that specifically target parity issues, aligning textures, behaviors, and mechanics to make Java behave more like Bedrock while keeping the experience **stable and consistent.**

The result is a **more coherent** experience for players transitioning between the two editions, reducing confusion and preserving the original feel of Bedrock.

![divider](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/b726dffef3bff5a52af4ab17734c700754958997_0.webp)

<br>![features](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/b166bc402e7aacbd3e4c6a09622c3ef217230866.png)

Why not just stick with Bedrock? The Bedrock edition may feel smoother out of the box, but it’s limited by its **closed ecosystem** and **lack of mod support.** Bedrock Reloaded runs on the powerful **Java** base, giving you real **customization**, mod **flexibility**, and advanced **performance** tuning — all while maintaining the familiar Bedrock feel.

### High Performance
- A **well-selected set** of performance-enhancing mods is included — improving the efficiency of internal calculations and game systems.
- Runs smoothly at **96 chunks** on Distant Horizons, even with shaders enabled.

### Stability
- Fixes **several bugs** and inconsistencies present in the Java edition.
- Unlike Bedrock, there is a way to add **mods** and other **free** additional content.

### Flexible
- Most of Bedrock's features can be **disabled** with a few clicks.
- **Server-friendly**, allowing you to quickly and easily set up.
- The modpack and many of the included mods are **open-source**.
